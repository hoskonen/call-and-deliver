# Call and Deliver

An immersive courier delivery mod for Skyrim Special Edition.

Call a courier using a craftable smoke signal at your campfire, hand over items, and pay to have them delivered to your home.

Deliveries take time and carry risks. Couriers may be delayed, robbed, or killed, leaving shipments to be recovered.

## Development

- **Target:** Skyrim AE 1.6.1170
- **Scripting:** Papyrus, SKSE, and Papyrus Extender where beneficial
- **Integration:** Campfire
- **Status:** Early development

The project prioritizes clean, maintainable code, compatibility, and immersive gameplay.
